03 — Reading the Dip

What held up, and where efficiency softened

No spin here — just the fuller picture behind the topline numbers.

● What held up
  • 92 real conversations generated across the two campaigns to date (50 in August, 42 in September) — a growing base of warm leads for NBK.
  • Reach grew 16.6% to 4,219 people and impressions grew 48% to 8,278 — more of the target audience saw the brand this cycle than last.
  • CPM held flat — ₹62.20 vs ₹63.24 in August — so the dip wasn't caused by a more expensive ad market.
  • At ₹12.26, cost per conversation is still 37% cheaper than the ₹19.58 NBK was paying per lead through self‑managed boosted posts before working with Spigle.
● Where it softened
  • Cost per conversation rose from ₹7.07 to ₹12.26, and total conversations dropped from 50 to 42, on 45.6% more spend.
  • Frequency climbed to 1.96 (from 1.55) — the same people were shown the ad nearly twice as often, a sign the audience wasn't given room to expand.
  • Click‑through rate softened from 3.52% to 2.96%, consistent with creative fatigue as frequency rose.
  • This campaign was greenlit and launched on a compressed timeline, leaving less runway than August for audience testing and creative rotation before launch.
04 — The Longer Trend

Still well ahead of where this account started

Conversations generated per ₹100 spent, across all three data points on record for NBK. September dipped from August's peak, but remains well above the pre‑Spigle baseline.

05 — Round Three

What changes from here

Concrete, testable changes for the next campaign — not just an explanation for this one.

01

Longer lead time

Lock the brief at least 7–10 days before launch so audience and creative testing isn't compressed into the flight itself.

02

Widen the audience

Expand targeting or add a lookalike segment to bring frequency back down from 1.96 and reduce repeat‑impression fatigue.

03

Refresh creative mid‑flight

Rotate in new creative once frequency crosses ~1.5 to keep CTR from sliding as the same ad repeats.
